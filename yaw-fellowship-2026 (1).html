Chapter One

Our vision

A continent where young African writers are heard, published and celebrated, and where our stories shape how Africa understands and imagines itself.”

The fellowship is small by design. Each cohort is reviewed from open submissions, read for voice and command of the form rather than credentials or prior publication.

Chapter Two

Our mission

To identify, fund and develop a small number of serious young writers each year, and hold them to a high standard of craft.

Identify. Submissions are read blind to background; the work is judged on its own terms.
Fund. Cost is removed as a factor, so admission is decided on the writing alone.
Develop. Mentorship and editorial feedback rigorous enough to change how a fellow writes.
Chapter Three

What we're working toward

The fellowship is built to outlast any single cohort.

A body of published work. Each cohort's strongest writing enters a growing anthology and essay collection, held to the same editorial standard as any other publication.
A standard, not a network. Fellows are chosen because the work holds up under scrutiny, and the fellowship is built to keep raising that bar.
A wider field to choose from. Full funding means selection is never limited to writers who can already afford to attend.

Chapter Six

What we hold to

  • Voice over polish

    Submissions are read for a distinct point of view before they're read for technique.

  • Rigor

    Feedback is direct. A fellow's draft is treated as work in progress, not a finished achievement.

  • Access on merit

    Full funding means the writing decides admission, not a fellow's means to attend.

  • Craft as the standard

    Editing here is exacting. Work that enters the anthology has been through several rounds of revision.

  • Cultural specificity

    African languages, histories and perspectives are treated as the subject, not a theme to explain to outsiders.

  • Ownership

    Fellows keep the rights to their own work. What we offer is editorial attention, not a claim on the writing.
